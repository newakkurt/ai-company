# First-Customer Pilot — NetCmd AI Network Copilot

**Offer status:** sales-ready pilot scope; **not a live checkout**. Confirm the payment tier and seller terms before accepting payment.

## The offer

A practical, evidence-led network health review for a small IT team or MSP that wants a repeatable monthly view of its network without granting an outside party unattended device access.

**Pilot price hypothesis:** USD 69/month for NetCmd PRO. This is the intended subscription price, not a verified active checkout price.

## Fixed pilot scope

- One customer site.
- Up to 10 network devices.
- Huawei, Cisco, and Aruba configuration or command-output evidence supplied by the customer.
- No direct device connection.
- No production configuration changes, shutdowns, deployments, or credential collection.
- Three business days after complete, sanitized evidence is received.
- One prioritized findings report, one remediation checklist, and one 30-minute review call.
- One clarification round on the delivered report.

## What the customer receives

1. **Inventory snapshot:** device name/role, vendor, model, software version where supplied, and evidence timestamp.
2. **Configuration review:** only checks supported by the supplied evidence; missing evidence is marked unknown, never assumed compliant.
3. **Risk-ranked findings:** critical/high/medium/low with evidence, operational impact, and suggested next step.
4. **Port and access review:** interface state, description, VLAN/mode, trunk/access context, and unused-port candidates where the evidence supports it.
5. **Security baseline:** AAA, management access, logging, NTP, SNMP version/security, port security, BPDU protection, DHCP snooping/DAI and related controls where relevant to the vendor and evidence.
6. **Management summary:** concise risks, quick wins, and a suggested 30-day action plan.

## Customer evidence checklist

The customer may provide sanitized text exports or screenshots for:

- Device inventory and topology context.
- Read-only interface and VLAN summaries.
- Relevant running configuration sections.
- Software/firmware versions.
- Existing monitoring or alert summaries, if available.

**Secrets removed:** passwords, API tokens, private keys, SNMP authentication/privacy secrets, public IPs that should remain private, personal data, and customer identifiers must be removed before sharing. Do not send credentials. If a secret appears in an upload, stop processing and request a sanitized replacement; do not reproduce the secret in a report.

## Boundaries and acceptance

- This is an evidence-based review, not a penetration test, certification, warranty, or guarantee that a network is secure.
- Findings are limited to the quality, age, and completeness of the supplied evidence.
- Recommendations require customer review and approval before implementation.
- No remote login, direct device connection, configuration push, or change execution is included.
- The customer confirms they own or are authorized to share the supplied network evidence.
- The customer should retain their own backups and change records.
