# Customer Onboarding — NetCmd AI Network Copilot

## Before the first audit
1. Confirm the customer's legal entity and authorized technical contact.
2. Define site, device count, vendor families and exclusions.
3. Obtain written authorization for read-only evidence collection.
4. Agree what configuration data may be uploaded and who can access it.
5. Remove credentials, secrets, public IPs where not needed, and personal data from shared evidence.
6. Confirm report delivery method, retention period and support contact.

## First-session checklist
- [ ] Customer goal and success criteria captured.
- [ ] Device inventory received.
- [ ] Vendor/software versions confirmed.
- [ ] Read-only evidence reviewed.
- [ ] Findings validated with customer context.
- [ ] Remediation priorities agreed.
- [ ] Any implementation work separately scoped and quoted.

## Customer-facing boundaries
NetCmd AI Network Copilot helps organize network evidence and produce actionable audit reports. It does not guarantee security, availability, regulatory compliance, or error-free configurations. No production change is performed without a separate written change approval.
